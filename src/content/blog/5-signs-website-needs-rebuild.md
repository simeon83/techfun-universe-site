A lot of business owners keep an old website running for years past its expiry date, mostly because rebuilding feels like a hassle. But an outdated site costs you more than it saves — in lost customers, lost search ranking, and a first impression that doesn't match how far your business has come.

### 1. It takes more than 3 seconds to load
Visitors decide whether to stay within the first few seconds. If your site is built on a heavy, outdated platform, that delay is likely costing you visitors before they ever see your content.

### 2. You can't update it yourself
If every text change requires calling a developer, your site can't keep up with your business. A modern setup should let you edit pages, prices, and blog posts yourself in a few clicks.

### 3. It doesn't show up on Google
Many older sites were never built with search visibility in mind. Basic technical SEO — page titles, meta descriptions, proper headings — makes a bigger difference than most people expect.

### 4. It looks broken on phones
Most of your visitors are on mobile. If your layout breaks, text overlaps, or buttons are too small to tap, you're losing them before they read a word.

### 5. You're embarrassed to share the link
This one's simple: if you hesitate before sending your own website to a potential client, that's the clearest sign of all.

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's probably time for a rebuild — and it doesn't have to mean starting from zero on cost or timeline.
